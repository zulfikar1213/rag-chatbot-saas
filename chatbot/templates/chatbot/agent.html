Lead Qualification Agent

An Agent that talks to your client, collects info during the chat, and if it understands the client needs RAG, it moves him to PDF Generation.

The Problem

You waste time with clients who don't want to buy. You talk 30 minutes, and then they say: I will think about it.

This Agent filters them for you. It asks the right questions, and only sends you the serious clients.

How It Works

Step 1: Agent asks

What is your business? Restaurant? Hotel? Clinic? Where? Najaf? Karbala? Qatar?

Step 2: Agent collects

It collects answers during the chat. No form. Just chat.

Step 3: Agent decides

If client needs RAG Chatbot, it moves him to PDF Generation. If not, it says thank you and stops.

Why Agent, Not Just Chatbot?

  • Loop: Plan -> Act -> Check. It plans what to ask, asks, checks if it understood.
  • Sub-Agents: One for RAG, one for PDF, one for Booking.
  • Guardrails: It never invents price. If it doesn't know, it says: I don't know.
